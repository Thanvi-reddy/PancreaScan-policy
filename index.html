Privacy Policy – PancreaScan

PancreaScan is a healthcare support application designed to assist healthcare professionals in reviewing pancreas CT scan images using Artificial Intelligence technologies.

Medical Disclaimer:
PancreaScan is intended only as a clinical support tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. AI-generated outputs must always be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional. Users should not rely solely on the application for medical decisions.

1. Information We Collect

PancreaScan may process the following information locally on the device:

  • CT scan images
  • Clinical annotations and notes
  • Patient history records
  • AI-generated analysis results

Patient data is primarily stored locally within the authorized clinical environment.

2. Federated Learning and AI Processing

PancreaScan uses federated learning technology to improve AI model performance while supporting patient privacy.

AI model training occurs locally on the device whenever applicable. Only encrypted and anonymized model updates or mathematical parameters may be securely transmitted to central servers.

Raw patient CT images and personally identifiable medical records are not intentionally uploaded as part of the federated learning process.

3. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to help protect stored medical and clinical information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

4. Intended Users

PancreaScan is intended only for trained healthcare professionals, researchers, and authorized clinical personnel.

The application is not intended for consumer self-diagnosis, self-treatment, or emergency medical use.

5. Clinical Decision Support

PancreaScan provides AI-assisted analysis to support clinical workflows. The application does not replace professional medical judgment, radiological interpretation, diagnosis, or treatment planning.

Always consult a licensed physician or qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

6. Offline Functionality

Certain analysis features may operate without an internet connection. Synchronization and AI model updates may require secure network access.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or operational changes.

Continued use of the application after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please contact your organization or application administrator.
